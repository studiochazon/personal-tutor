# Sustainable Energy: A Comprehensive Course

## Course Overview
This course provides a thorough introduction to sustainable energy technologies, their applications, and their role in addressing climate change. Students will explore renewable energy sources, energy efficiency, and the transition to a sustainable energy future.

## Course Outline

### Module 1: Introduction to Sustainable Energy
- What is sustainable energy?
- The environmental and economic case for renewable energy
- Global energy consumption patterns
- Climate change and energy policy

### Module 2: Solar Energy
- Photovoltaic technology
- Solar thermal systems
- Grid integration challenges
- Cost trends and market development

### Module 3: Wind Energy
- Wind turbine technology
- Onshore vs offshore wind farms
- Environmental considerations
- Wind resource assessment

### Module 4: Hydroelectric Power
- Large-scale hydroelectric projects
- Small-scale hydro systems
- Environmental impacts
- Future of hydroelectric energy

### Module 5: Other Renewable Sources
- Geothermal energy
- Biomass and biofuels
- Ocean energy (tidal and wave)
- Emerging technologies

### Module 6: Energy Storage and Grid Integration
- Battery technologies
- Pumped hydro storage
- Smart grid systems
- Grid stability and renewable integration

### Module 7: Energy Efficiency and Conservation
- Building efficiency standards
- Industrial energy management
- Transportation electrification
- Behavioral changes for energy conservation

### Module 8: Policy and Economics
- Government incentives and regulations
- Carbon pricing mechanisms
- Investment in renewable energy
- Social and economic impacts

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this course, students will:
- Understand the technical fundamentals of major renewable energy technologies
- Analyze the economic and environmental benefits of sustainable energy
- Evaluate policy approaches to promoting renewable energy adoption
- Design basic renewable energy systems for specific applications

## Assessment Methods
- Weekly quizzes on technical concepts
- Case study analysis assignments
- Group project designing a renewable energy system
- Final comprehensive examination

## Prerequisites
- Basic understanding of physics and mathematics
- Introductory knowledge of environmental science (recommended)

## Duration
12 weeks, 3 hours per week (36 total contact hours)
